Five worlds and a song, all free. Start with the City or jump straight to the stars.

      

      

        
        
        
      

      

        

          

            

            

              Free
              
City

              
The classic contribution graph, raised into a skyline. One bar per day, from a whisper of green to your biggest day.

              
  • Eight color themes, GitHub green included
  • Website embed and README image
  • Download as an STL for 3D printing

              
Make it mine →

Night City

              
Your year after dark. Big days become glass towers, quiet days lit blocks, with traffic running down the avenues.

              
  • Glowing windows by contribution level
  • Cars, street lights and rooftop beacons
  • STL export of the whole skyline

              
Make it mine →

Galaxy

              
January at the core, December at the rim. Every day is a star, and your biggest days become planets.

  • Slowly turning on its own
